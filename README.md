## 📖 Overview
**DataPilot** is a high-performance, native macOS application engineered for Data Analysts and Business Intelligence professionals. Designed to eliminate the friction of cloud-based BI tools, it leverages the raw processing power of **Apple Silicon (M4)** for heavy, local data crunching. Zero latency, complete data privacy, and instant actionable insights right on your desktop.

---

## ✨ Core Innovations

### ⚡ 1. Local Heavy Processing (Cloud-Free)
- Executes complex SQL joins, data aggregation, and structural validation entirely on-device.
- Designed to handle massive datasets seamlessly without relying on external cloud infrastructure.
- **Privacy First:** Your proprietary business data never leaves your machine.

### 🌐 2. Dual-Language Insight Engine (English / Hinglish)
- **Dynamic Toggle:** Instantly switch auto-generated executive insights between professional **English** and highly accessible **Hinglish** with a single click.
- Makes analytics reporting faster, highly intuitive, and perfectly tailored for diverse Indian stakeholders and regional teams.

### 📊 3. Automated Executive Reporting
- Generate branded, one-click PDF reports featuring custom KPIs, localized insights, and root-cause analysis directly from your imported datasets.

---

## 💻 Tech Stack & Architecture
- **Core Frameworks:** SwiftUI, Foundation, CoreData / SQLite
- **Data Handling:** Custom Swift-based data-processing algorithms optimized for M-series chips.
- **Architecture Pattern:** MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel) for scalable UI and logic separation.
